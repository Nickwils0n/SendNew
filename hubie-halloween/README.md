# Hubie's Halloween Ride

A pixel art side-scrolling bike game based on the movie *Hubie Halloween* (2020).
It's one self-contained file. Open `index.html` in any modern browser; no build or install needed.

Hubie rides through Salem on Halloween, blasting pranksters in costumes with his thermos.
He jumps obstacles, lands bike tricks, recruits pals, and spends candy corn on thermos gadgets.
The six levels follow the movie:

1. **Halloween Morning**: ride to the deli while the town pelts Hubie with eggs. Violet joins.
2. **The Halloween Helper**: Richie Hartman has escaped; meet new neighbor Walter. Boss: Mike.
3. **The Corn Maze**: Mike vanishes in the corn. Sgt. Downey joins.
4. **The Spooky Road**: tail the Hennesseys past the drive-in. Richie joins. Boss: the Hennesseys' wagon.
5. **The Haunted House**: the fun fair, a husky named Buster, and a dark haunted house. Boss: Pete.
6. **The Kidnapper**: free the prisoners tied to the trees and unmask the kidnapper.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ride | ← → or A D | D-pad / left stick |
| Jump (hold for higher) | Space, Z, ↑ or W | A |
| Shoot thermos (hold) | X or J | X / RT |
| Trick (in the air, add ↑ ↓ ← → for different tricks) | C or K | B / RB |
| Hubie Holler (when Courage is full) | V or L | Y / LB |
| Pause / Mute | P or Esc / M | Start |

On phones and tablets, on-screen buttons appear automatically.

Landing tricks earns points, candy corn and Courage. Landing mid-trick is a bail, and you drop candy.
Stomp enemies and pumpkins from above. Between levels, spend candy corn at Hubie's Thermos Workbench.
Its upgrades are hotter soup, rapid pour, spread spout, flare gun, drill bit, umbrella, grappling hook,
vacuum, inhaler, megaphone, flashlight and blender smoothie.

Progress saves after each level in the browser's local storage.

Fan-made tribute; not affiliated with Netflix or Happy Madison. All art and sound are generated in code.
